# Mission

## Why This Exists

AI is becoming the most powerful technology in human history. Right now it belongs to a handful of corporations and governments. They decide what the model can say, who gets access, and what it costs. This is a civilizational risk.

## What We're Building

A language model that belongs to no one and everyone.

- **No company in the middle.** The network IS the participants.
- **No one controls the model.** No censorship, no kill switch, no "sorry, I can't help with that because our policy says so."
- **Free for everyone.** You give compute, you get AI. Your phone is both the server and the client.
- **The more people join, the smarter it gets.** Every phone adds a specialist brain. Every keystroke teaches the model.

## How It Works

Split the AI model into parallel pieces. Each piece runs on someone's phone. Phones compute simultaneously and results are merged. No single point of failure, no central server needed.

```
Traditional AI:
  Corporation owns model → runs on their servers → you pay → they control

Our AI:
  Everyone owns a piece → runs on everyone's phones → free → no one controls
```

## Core Principles

1. **Decentralized by architecture, not by ideology.** The model literally cannot be controlled by one entity because it physically exists across millions of phones.

2. **Free as in freedom AND free as in beer.** Use your phone's idle compute → get AI for free. No subscription, no API key, no waitlist.

3. **Compute Miles belong to users.** Earn miles by contributing compute. Spend them, trade them, give them away. We don't take a cut.

4. **Privacy by default.** Signals-only mode: we see "model predicted X, user typed Y" — not what you actually typed. Full data sharing is opt-in with bonus miles.

5. **Open.** Architecture, training code, model weights — all open. Anyone can verify, fork, or improve.

## Self-Improving by Design (Gödel-Darwin-Cybernetic)

This is not a model that we train and ship. It's a system that **cannot stop improving**.

**Gödel (formal self-modification):** The model rewrites its own weights every day. Users type → signals flow → federated learning adjusts the model → predictions improve → more accepted predictions → more signals → cycle never stops. No human in the loop.

**Darwin (mutation + selection):** Tracks specialize randomly based on their user's data. A doctor's track learns medicine. A programmer's track learns code. Router sends more traffic to better tracks (more miles). Worse tracks get less traffic and fade. Natural selection — no one manages it.

**Cybernetic (self-correction through feedback loops):**
- Milliseconds: prediction accepted/rejected → instant signal
- Days: weekly federated averaging → model update
- Months: track reputation shifts → router rebalances
- Years: new tracks emerge, old ones die → ecosystem evolves

Four feedback loops at different speeds, all pulling the system toward better. The system physically cannot degrade for long — the loops correct it.

**Why this matters:** OpenAI trains a model, ships it, it's frozen until next version. Our model gets smarter every hour of every day, powered by the people who use it.

## The Endgame

When AI is as distributed as the internet itself, no government can shut it down, no corporation can paywall it, and no one can decide what it's allowed to think.
